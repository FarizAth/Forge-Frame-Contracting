HOW WE WORK

A Clear, Structured Construction Process

No guessing, no hidden fees, and no dropped communication. Here is how your project progresses from initial concept to final walkthrough.

01

Talk

We start with an on-site consultation at your Salt Lake property. We review your goals, budget realistic ranges, assess structural requirements, and discuss initial feasibility.

02

Plan

Detailed architectural drawings, material selections, trade scheduling, and line-item budgeting occur here. You approve every spec before a hammer touches a nail.

03

Build

Demolition, framing, mechanical updates, and finish work happen systematically. Our site leads keep the workspace clean and update you with direct progress reports.

04

Finish

Tile setting, trim carpentry, hardware installation, and detailed surface finishes are executed to exact tolerances. We inspect every edge with a fine-tooth comb.

05

Walkthrough

We review the completed work together, resolve any punch-list items immediately, hand over warranty documents, and leave your home spotless and ready to enjoy.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear information regarding project costs, scheduling, and contractor selection in Salt Lake City.

Residential project costs vary according to square footage, structural changes, and finish grades. In the Salt Lake market, kitchen remodels typically range between $45,000 and $120,000, primary bathroom suite renovations run between $30,000 and $75,000, and major whole-home additions or structural reconfigurations often range from $120,000 to $250,000+.

Timeline depends directly on project scope and material lead times. A dedicated kitchen remodel generally spans 5 to 8 weeks of active site work, while larger structural additions or whole-home reconfigurations typically range between 10 and 18 weeks. Detailed timelines are provided in our initial planning phase.

It is best to reach out 2 to 4 months before your desired construction start date. This window allows ample time for preliminary site evaluations, design iteration, permit approval through your local municipal building department, and material ordering.

Yes. Forge & Frame handles all required building permits and coordinates mandatory municipal inspections (framing, electrical, plumbing, insulation, and final occupancy) with Salt Lake County or local city building divisions.

For localized renovations such as a single bathroom or kitchen update, many homeowners remain in the home comfortably. For full-home interior transformations or major multi-room additions, temporary temporary living arrangements may be recommended for safety and convenience.
